Introduction

This data set shows the rates of traumatic brain injury related visits to the ED, hospitalizations, and deaths by sex in the U.S. from 2001 to 2010. This report will have descriptive statistics summarizing these rates by sex and by year, including overall trends and sex-based rate comparisons across the decade. To visualize these patterns, a line graph will be produced showing two lines by sex plotted over the years 2001 to 2010.
